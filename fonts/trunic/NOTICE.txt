Tunic font

Original script by Andrew Shouldice for the game Tunic.
Font by Adrián Jiménez Pascual (dirdam.github.io).

Source: https://github.com/dirdam/fonts/tree/main/tunic
Original font: https://github.com/dirdam/fonts/blob/main/tunic/Tunic.otf
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original OTF is distributed unchanged. The website uses a WOFF2 derivative
produced by its font-subsetting pipeline; glyph shapes are not edited.
Some punctuation, numbers and symbols are the font author's extensions rather
than characters in the original game.
